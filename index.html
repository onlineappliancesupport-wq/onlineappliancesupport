What we do

Clear technical guidance before you spend money on a repair.

Share your appliance details, symptoms, and any error code. We help you understand the likely fault and the safest next step through clear remote guidance.

  • ✓Fault and error-code guidance
    We break down what the information points to and what it may mean for the appliance.
  • ✓Safe model-specific checks
    Where appropriate, we guide basic checks, resets, and available diagnostic or test modes.
  • ✓Repair-value decision
    We help you decide whether repair is likely worthwhile based on the information available.
  • ✓Parts guidance
    When relevant, we explain likely parts and compare indicative prices for genuine and compatible replacement parts.
Safety first

Remote guidance has clear limits.

We do not instruct customers to carry out hazardous electrical, gas, sealed refrigeration-system, or other unsafe work.
